How the numbers are calculated

The APR on this site follows the Stonk Board's published methodology for transfer-tax coins. Every input is public and linked below. The code and every 15-minute snapshot are on GitHub.

APR

APR = fees assessed × (1 − deduction) ÷ days ÷ eligible holdings × 365 × 100

Fees and holdings are both counted in KSHAPE and valued at the same price, so the price cancels. The APR is the share of the eligible supply paid out as tax, per year. Changes in KSHAPE's own price are not part of it; the Board leaves them out the same way.

Windows

  • Since launch: from launch to the latest snapshot.
  • 24h, 3d, 7d: the last 24, 72 and 168 complete hours, which are the Board's windows. Each shows n/a until that much history exists.
  • Realized: NVDAX actually paid plus pending, at today's NVDA price, annualized over the time since launch and divided by eligible holdings at today's KSHAPE price.

APY

APY = 100 × [(1 + APR ÷ 36,500)^365 − 1]

The Board's formula: one reinvestment a day at a constant APR.

Where this differs from the Stonk Board

  • The Board lists a coin only after it has been bonded for 7 days, and every window must start after bonding. KSHAPE hasn't bonded, so the Board doesn't list it yet. This site includes pre-bond data.
  • "Since launch" is this site's window. The Board publishes 24h, 3d and 7d.
  • The Board assigns each fee to the transfer that paid it. This site assigns fees to when the platform withdrew them, usually minutes to a few hours later. Totals are identical; a window's edges can shift by one withdrawal.
  • The Board applies StonkFun's configured operating deduction. StonkFun doesn't publish that figure, so this site measures it from the chain.
